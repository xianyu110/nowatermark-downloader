Audio research often crosses formats. A team may collect a TikTok sound, a YouTube clip, a music track, an X video quote, or an Instagram reel. The workflow should keep source URLs, metadata, and transcripts in one place.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com) for mixed links, [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader) for music links, and [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) when spoken words need to become text.

## Research checklist

Capture the original URL, platform, creator or artist, project, permission note, metadata, and exported file name. If the asset includes speech, export TXT or SRT so editors can search and quote it.

## Scaling the workflow

For recurring research, connect parsing to [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs). Store successes and failures so the team knows which links were processed, which were unavailable, and which need manual review.
