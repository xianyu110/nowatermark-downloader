Repurposing starts with understanding the source. A public Facebook video may be useful as a quote, tutorial reference, customer example, or campaign input.

Parse the media at [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader), then use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) if the spoken content matters.

## Repurpose steps

Save the source URL, parse the public video, generate a transcript, extract key points, and document permission before using the material externally.

## What not to do

Do not treat downloads as permission. Do not remove attribution. Do not process private or login-only videos. The workflow should improve review speed without creating rights problems.
