## Best available does not mean guaranteed

“Best available” means the workflow requests the highest accessible output from the parser chain. The final result may depend on the post type, platform delivery, provider behavior, region, and account restrictions on the source platform.

## When to choose lower quality

Lower quality can be better for quick review, small archives, internal notes, and mobile sharing. High quality is more useful for editing, screenshots, presentation clips, and long-term archives.

## Keep the source URL

Always store the original TikTok URL beside the exported file. If a better output becomes available later, the source record lets you retry the same public post.
