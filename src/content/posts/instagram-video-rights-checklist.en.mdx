A downloader makes public media easier to handle, but it does not grant reuse rights. Teams should check permissions before saving or republishing Instagram videos.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/instagram-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/instagram-downloader) for technical parsing, and keep rights review as a separate workflow.

## Checklist

- Is the post public?
- Do we own the content or have permission?
- Is the intended use internal research, editing, reporting, or publication?
- Do we need attribution?
- Do we need to remove the file after the project?

## Keep proof with the file

Store the original Instagram URL, creator handle, permission note, capture date, and reviewer. This reduces confusion when the file is shared with editors or clients.

## Use transcripts carefully

If you use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe), review the transcript before quoting or publishing it. Automated text can miss names, slang, and context.
