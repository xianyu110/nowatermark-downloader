Social listening is not only text search. Many useful reactions, complaints, demos, and testimonials appear inside short videos. A public Twitter or X clip can contain the evidence a team needs, but it must be handled with source context.

Collect public links and process them with [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/twitter-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/twitter-video-downloader). For mixed platform monitoring, use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com).

## What to capture

Save the original URL, author handle, topic, date, campaign, and a short summary. If the video includes spoken feedback, use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) to create searchable text.

## Why transcript matters

Text allows researchers to cluster themes, find repeated complaints, extract quotes for internal reports, and translate findings for regional teams. It also reduces the time spent replaying clips.
