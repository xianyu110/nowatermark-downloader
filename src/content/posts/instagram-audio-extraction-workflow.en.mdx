The important part of an Instagram video is often the audio: spoken hooks, testimonials, product claims, or background music.

## When audio helps

Audio extraction is useful for speech review, localization, podcast notes, music identification, and subtitle preparation. It helps teams review what was said without replaying the entire video.

## Keep rights clear

Audio can include copyrighted songs and creator voice. Downloading or extracting audio does not create permission. Keep the public source URL and usage note with every export.
