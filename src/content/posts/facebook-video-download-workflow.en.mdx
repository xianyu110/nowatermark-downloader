Facebook videos appear in pages, groups, reels, profiles, and shared posts. A clean workflow starts by confirming that the video is public and that your team has a valid reason to process it.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader) for Facebook-specific links, or start from [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com) when the queue contains mixed platforms.

## Recommended process

Copy the full public Facebook video URL, paste it into the downloader, and review the returned media details. If a result is available, save the exported file next to the source URL, creator or page name, capture date, and permission note.

## When to add text

If the video contains a testimonial, tutorial, or campaign message, use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) after parsing. Searchable text makes Facebook video research easier to summarize and reuse.
