Batch downloading is useful when a team reviews many public TikTok clips each day. The risk is losing control of source records, duplicate links, and failed parses.

For TikTok-specific work, start with [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tiktok-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tiktok-downloader). For mixed links, use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com).

## Prepare the link list

Remove duplicate URLs, keep one link per line, and add a short note beside each source in your spreadsheet. A good batch list includes author, campaign, reviewer, intended use, and permission status.

## Parse in small batches

Small batches are easier to debug. When a group contains too many different platforms, failed links become harder to isolate. Split TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube links into separate lists when possible.

Batch parsing and advanced exports are paid workflow features. Check [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/pricing](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/pricing) when the team needs more volume, audio-only output, best quality selection, or API access.

## Handle failed items

Do not delete failed rows. Mark them as private, unavailable, unsupported, or retry later. This keeps the review queue honest and prevents the same bad link from being pasted repeatedly.

If the goal is research or repurposing, send successful media into [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe). A transcript is easier to search, summarize, translate, and approve than a raw video.
