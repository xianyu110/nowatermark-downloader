## When audio extraction is useful

Audio extraction helps with podcast notes, quote review, voiceover analysis, sound research, subtitle preparation, and localization. It is also useful when an editor needs to separate the spoken content from the visual context.

## Rights and attribution

Audio can carry music, voice, and copyrighted material. Downloading or extracting audio does not grant permission to reuse it. Keep the original TikTok URL, creator name, and permission note with the exported file.
