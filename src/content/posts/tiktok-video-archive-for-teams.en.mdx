A useful TikTok archive is more than a folder of video files. It is a structured record of public links, context, permissions, exports, and review status.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tiktok-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tiktok-downloader) when the source is TikTok and [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com) when the queue contains mixed platforms.

## Archive fields

Track the original URL, platform, creator handle, capture date, parser status, exported filename, transcript filename, usage rights, and removal status. These fields make the archive searchable and safer to maintain.

## File naming

Avoid generic names such as `video-final.mp4`. Use a pattern that includes platform, creator, date, and a short content label. The goal is to make files understandable after the browser tab is gone.

## Review process

Before a video moves into editing, confirm that it is public and that your team has a valid reason to store or reuse it. If the video is part of a campaign, keep permission notes next to the file.
