A folder full of downloaded clips is not a real archive. A useful Twitter or X video archive needs source records, dates, authors, rights notes, and a clear deletion process.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/twitter-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/twitter-video-downloader) for public video links. Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) when the archive also needs searchable text.

## Minimum fields to save

Save the original URL, author handle, capture date, campaign or research project, exported filename, parser status, transcript status, and permission note. If the file is used in a public asset, also store the final destination URL.

## Folder structure

Group clips by project, platform, and month. Use clear filenames such as `2026-08-x-author-topic.mp4`. Put transcripts beside the media file so editors can search text without opening the video.

For automated archives, use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs) and store both successful downloads and failed parse attempts. Failed records are useful because they explain gaps in the archive.
