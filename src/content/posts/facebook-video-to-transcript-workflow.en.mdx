Many Facebook videos contain spoken content that is more useful as text than as a raw media file. A transcript helps teams search, quote, summarize, and localize public videos.

First parse the public video at [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader). Then continue with [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) when media is available.

## Suggested workflow

Save the original Facebook URL, parse the media, generate a transcript, and store the transcript beside the source link. Add channel, date, reviewer, and permission notes.

## Review carefully

Automatic transcripts can miss names, slang, and overlapping speech. Use the output as a draft, then review it before publishing or quoting.
