YouTube videos often contain long explanations, interviews, tutorials, and podcasts. Audio extraction can make review faster when the visual track is less important.

## When audio is useful

Audio helps with podcast notes, quote extraction, subtitle planning, localization, and internal review. It reduces the time needed to replay long videos during research.

## Keep source context

Audio files can become disconnected from their source. Store the original YouTube URL, channel, title, capture date, and permission note beside every export.
