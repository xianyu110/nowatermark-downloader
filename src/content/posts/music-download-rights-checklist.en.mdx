Music rights are stricter than many short video workflows. A parser can help retrieve public metadata or available media, but it does not give permission to reuse a song commercially.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader) only for links you are allowed to process.

## Rights checklist

Do you own the track? Do you have a license? Is the use internal research, reference, commentary, or a platform-permitted workflow? Does the final asset require attribution, platform usage, or deletion after review?

## Record keeping

Save the original URL, platform, title, artist, parse date, permission note, and final use. If a file is used in an external campaign, store the license or approval reference next to it.

For API workflows, include permission and retention fields before processing through [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs).
