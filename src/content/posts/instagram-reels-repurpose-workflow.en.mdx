## Repurpose steps

1. Save the original public Instagram URL.
2. Parse the media at [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com).
3. Generate a transcript for speech-heavy clips.
4. Extract hooks, claims, examples, and CTA patterns.
5. Document permission before any external reuse.

## What not to do

Do not treat downloads as automatic permission. Do not remove attribution. Do not use private or login-only content. A responsible workflow protects the creator and the team.
