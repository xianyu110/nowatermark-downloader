A downloader does not grant reuse rights. Before processing a public Facebook video, confirm why the content is being saved and how it will be used.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader) for parsing, but keep rights review separate.

## Checklist

- Is the video public?
- Do you own it or have permission?
- Is the intended use internal review or external publication?
- Is attribution required?
- Should the file be removed after the project?

## Keep proof

Store the source URL, page name, permission note, capture date, and reviewer. If a rights holder asks for removal, the archive can be searched quickly.
