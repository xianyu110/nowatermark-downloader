Channel research creates many files quickly. Without a clear archive structure, public YouTube downloads become difficult to search and review.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/youtube-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/youtube-downloader) for individual videos and Shorts, then store every export with source context.

## Archive fields

Track channel name, source URL, title, capture date, exported filename, transcript filename, topic, reviewer, and permission status.

## Folder strategy

Organize by channel, project, or research topic. Keep the source URL in a spreadsheet or metadata file so the video can be audited later.

## Transcript strategy

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) for important videos. Text makes channel research easier to search and compare.

## Mixed media queues

When research includes TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube together, start from [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com) and separate failed links by platform.
