Video quality depends on what Facebook and the parser provider can expose for a public post. A downloader cannot create a real higher-quality file that is not available from the source.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader) and inspect the returned media result.

## Choose by use case

Lower quality can be enough for quick review and research notes. Best available output is better for editing, presentations, and long-term archives.

## Keep the source URL

Always store the original public Facebook URL. If a better output becomes available later, your team can retry the same source.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) when the spoken content matters more than resolution.
