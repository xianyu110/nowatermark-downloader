Music workflows often need metadata more than a file. A creator may need the song title, artist, album, cover image, or source platform before planning a video, playlist, or research note.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/music-downloader) to parse public music links from supported platforms.

## Metadata fields to keep

Save the original URL, platform, title, artist, album, cover URL, duration if available, returned audio status, and parse time. If the link is part of a larger video project, store the project name too.

## Why source records matter

Music availability can change. A track may be removed, restricted, or updated. Keeping the original URL beside the metadata gives your team a way to audit where a result came from.
