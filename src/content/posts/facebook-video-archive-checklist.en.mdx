A Facebook video archive is only useful when every file can be traced back to its source. Downloads without URLs become hard to audit.

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader) to process public Facebook links, then store metadata with each export.

## Minimum archive fields

Track source URL, page or profile, title, capture date, exported filename, transcript status, reviewer, campaign, and permission note.

## Keep source context

Facebook posts can be edited, deleted, or restricted later. The source URL and capture date explain what your team saw at the time of processing.

## Add transcripts

Use [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/transcribe) for videos that contain speech. Transcripts make the archive searchable and easier to summarize.
