Quality selection depends on what the public platform response and parser provider can return. A downloader cannot guarantee a format that is not available from the source.

Start with [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/youtube-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/youtube-downloader), paste a public URL, and inspect the output options.

## Choose by use case

Use lower quality for quick review, research notes, and small archives. Use best available output for editing, presentation, screenshots, and long-term storage.

## Keep originals

Always save the original YouTube URL. If a better result is available later, the team can retry the same public source.
