Facebook download failures usually come from access limits, unsupported formats, or temporary provider issues. The first step is to confirm the video is public.

Open [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/tools/facebook-video-downloader) and paste the full URL. If it fails, test the source in a browser.

## Common causes

- The post is private, deleted, group-only, or login-only.
- The link points to a feed page instead of a direct video post.
- A provider is rate limited or temporarily unavailable.
- The copied URL includes punctuation from a message.

## What to do next

Copy the full public URL again, retry later, and test another public Facebook video. If the same pattern repeats, log the error instead of repeatedly pasting the same failed link.

Structured retries are easier with [https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs](https://nowatermarkdownloader.com/api-docs).
