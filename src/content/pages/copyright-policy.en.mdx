## Respect for Rights

NoWatermark Downloader is intended for public content that the user owns, is authorized to use, or may lawfully process. The service does not grant permission to copy, redistribute, or commercially exploit third-party content.

## How the Service Works

The service accepts a public page URL and sends it to configured third-party parsing providers. It may return a direct media URL supplied by those providers. We do not claim ownership of source videos and do not routinely host copies of the downloaded media on our servers.

## Reporting a Copyright Concern

Rights holders or their authorized representatives may email [support@nowatermarkdownloader.com](mailto:support@nowatermarkdownloader.com) with:

- Identification of the copyrighted work.
- The exact page or media URL involved.
- Contact details for the rights holder or representative.
- A statement explaining the ownership or authorization to act.
- A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized.
- A statement that the information supplied is accurate.

Please do not include unrelated personal data or account passwords.

## Our Response

We may investigate the report, restrict abusive accounts, block specific URLs or domains, preserve relevant records where legally required, and cooperate with valid legal requests. Because source media is usually hosted by a third-party platform, permanent removal may also require contacting that platform or its hosting provider.

## Misrepresentation

Knowingly submitting a false or misleading report may create legal liability. If you believe a restriction was made in error, contact us with the relevant URLs, account details, and an explanation of your authority to use the content.
